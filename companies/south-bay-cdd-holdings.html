    $3.1 million

    Community Development District (special taxing district for a new community)

    Approve, by supermajority vote, a contract with South Bay CDD Holdings, Inc. a Florida corporation, to acquire approximately 73.73 acres for $3,137,000 located within the Bahia Beach Coastal Restoration site

    • Size: about 73.73 acres
    • Money: $3.1 million
    What does “Community Development District” mean?

    A special district a developer can set up under Florida law (Chapter 190) to borrow money for a new community's roads, water and amenities. Homeowners there repay it through an extra yearly charge on their property tax bill. Source. More

    Official wording

    Approve, by supermajority vote, a contract with South Bay CDD Holdings, Inc. a Florida corporation, to acquire approximately 73.73 acres for $3,137,000 located within the Bahia Beach Coastal Restoration site... Made the motion to approve the acquisition.

    Passed 7 to 0.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2021 voting record, page 35. Look for roll call (RCS) 2760.
