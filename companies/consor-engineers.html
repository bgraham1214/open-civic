These are payments actually sent, read from the Clerk's weekly payment registers. The decisions above are permissions to spend up to an amount. The Clerk keeps about eight weeks of registers online; we have 8 weeks, ending September 27, 2026 through November 15, 2026, so an earlier payment isn't here, and no payment here doesn't mean none was made. Payees the Clerk blacked out are left out.
