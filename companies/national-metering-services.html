These are payments actually sent, read from the Clerk's weekly payment registers. The decisions above are permissions to spend up to an amount. The registers don't say which decision a payment belongs to; a payment is shown beside a decision only when the amounts are the same, and that match is the machine's, not the Clerk's. The Clerk keeps about eight weeks of registers online; we have 8 weeks, ending September 27, 2026 through November 15, 2026, so an earlier payment isn't here, and no payment here doesn't mean none was made. Payees the Clerk blacked out are left out.
