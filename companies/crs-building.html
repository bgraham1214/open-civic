CRS Building Corp

Public money approved for this company or organization

Hillsborough approved $9.3 million for CRS Building Corp in 3 decisions, from September 4, 2014 to April 7, 2021.

Amounts are what a board approved, usually a contract's ceiling, not money paid. How this works.

Also written as: CRS Building Corp.; CRS Building Corporation.
