Payments Collier County's Clerk recorded

The Collier County Clerk of the Circuit Court & Comptroller recorded $759,250.00 in 12 payment lines to REMI Companies, LLC, paid from July 14, 2025 to November 19, 2025.

These are payments actually sent, read from the Disbursements Reports the Clerk files with the County Commission's agenda about every two weeks. We have the 46 lists filed with agendas from January 14, 2025 to November 10, 2026, covering payments made from December 2, 2024 to October 28, 2026; a payment outside those dates isn't here. The decisions above are permissions to spend up to an amount. The lists don't say which decision a payment belongs to; a payment is shown beside a decision only when the amounts are the same, and that match is the machine's, not the Clerk's. No payment here doesn't mean none was made. Payees the Clerk blacked out are left out.
