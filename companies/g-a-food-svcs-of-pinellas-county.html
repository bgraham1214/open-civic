G.A. Food Svcs of Pinellas County Inc.

Public money approved for this company or organization

Hillsborough approved $3 million for G.A. Food Svcs of Pinellas County Inc. in 2 decisions, from February 18, 2015 to September 16, 2015.

Amounts are what a board approved, usually a contract's ceiling, not money paid. How this works.
