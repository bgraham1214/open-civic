    $2.1 million

    Contract or purchase: $2.1 million

    Award & execute an agrmt w/ lowest responsive & responsible bidder, Proshot Concrete Inc. in the amt of $2.1 million to provide, on an as-needed basis, misc. bridge repair & rehab svcs for Public Works

    What does “Contract or purchase” mean?

    The county agrees to pay a company or group for goods or work. ITB (invitation to bid) and RFP (request for proposals) are the ways the county asks companies to compete for the job. More
