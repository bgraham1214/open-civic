These are payments actually sent, read from the Disbursements Reports the Clerk files with the County Commission's agenda about every two weeks. The decisions above are permissions to spend up to an amount. The lists don't say which decision a payment belongs to; a payment is shown beside a decision only when the amounts are the same, and that match is the machine's, not the Clerk's. No payment here doesn't mean none was made. Payees the Clerk blacked out are left out.
