Jacksonville Beach · Neptune Beach (Duval County)

Jacksonville Beach and Neptune Beach each have their own city government. City councils and commissions decide zoning, city services and city taxes inside city limits. Other governments in Duval County aren't here yet: see what we cover in Florida.
