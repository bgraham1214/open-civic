What we cover in Florida

Florida has 67 counties and 412 cities, towns and villages, each with its own elected board. We collect 117 of those 479 governments so far.

A government is here when its meeting records are on a system our software can already read (Legistar or CivicClerk; a few have their own reader) and that system doesn't ask automated tools to stay out. The others publish on systems we haven't built a reader for yet, or only as documents on their own websites. "Not collected yet" says nothing about how open a government is.

If you live outside city limits, your county government is your local government. The list of governments is the U.S. Census Bureau's (2020).
