Charlotte County

The Charlotte County government decides zoning, roads and county taxes outside city limits, and county-wide services everywhere. Other governments in Charlotte County aren't here yet: see what we cover in Florida.

What we collect from Charlotte County

Board of County Commissioners meetings and other public boards, from Charlotte County's Legistar records, with agenda items, actions and each member's vote where the record gives one, from 2025. Hearings about private residents' own cases (code enforcement, special magistrates, value adjustment boards) are left out. Agendas and minutes are linked on the government's own site.
