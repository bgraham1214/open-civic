What these words mean

City Council
The elected board that runs city government: the mayor and the council members. It decides the city's laws, budget, taxes, zoning and contracts. In Temple Terrace the mayor votes too.
Motion
A formal proposal by one member for what the council should do. Another member seconds it, then the council votes.
Consent agenda
A group of routine items approved together in one vote, to save time. Any member can ask to pull an item off it for a separate discussion and vote.
Resolution
A formal decision of the council, such as approving a contract or a purchase.
Ordinance
A city law. It is usually read and voted on at two meetings (first and second reading) before it takes effect.
Didn't vote
The member was absent from the meeting, or the minutes don't record their vote.
