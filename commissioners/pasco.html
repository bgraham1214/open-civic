Pasco County Commission

How each commissioner voted

Wesley Chapel doesn't have a city government. Decisions about zoning, roads and county taxes there are made by these 5 commissioners.

Records through October 20, 2026. Counts come from official vote records and say nothing about why anyone voted the way they did.

Votes read from the Pasco Clerk's minutes (County Commission meetings from 2025 on). The Clerk names only members who voted no, abstained or were absent; everyone else present is counted as yes. Items whose minutes we couldn't read with confidence (several motions on one item, for example) are left out.
