What these words mean

County Commission
The elected board that runs county government. In places without a city government of their own, like Spring Hill, it makes the local decisions: zoning, roads, parks, the budget and county taxes.
Motion
A formal proposal by one commissioner for what the board should do. “Moved approval” means a commissioner proposed approving the item. The board then votes on it.
Consent agenda
A group of routine items approved together in one vote, to save time.
Rezoning
Changing the rules for what can be built on a piece of land. Codes like PRS, RZ and MM are the county's case numbers for these requests.
Long-range land plan
The county's Comprehensive Plan: a map and rules for how land can be used in the future. Changing it usually comes before a rezoning.
Vacating petition
A request for the county to give up a public road, alley or easement it no longer needs.
Didn't vote
The member was recorded as absent, excused, recused or not voting.
