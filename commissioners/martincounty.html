How each commissioner voted

These 5 commissioners make Martin County's county-wide decisions, and the local decisions (zoning, roads, county taxes) for everyone who lives outside a city.

Records through November 10, 2026. Counts come from official vote records and say nothing about why anyone voted the way they did.
