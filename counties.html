County commission records

Brandon and Lutz (Hillsborough) · Wesley Chapel (Pasco) · Spring Hill (Hernando)

Brandon, Lutz, Wesley Chapel and Spring Hill have no city government of their own. Their local decisions are made by their county commission, so we follow those commissions.

What we collect from Hillsborough County

Every roll-call vote of the County Commission since January 2014, from the Clerk's Master Voting Record (updated monthly by the Clerk, so the newest meetings appear a few weeks late; some 2016-2017 pages are scans, and 33 rows we couldn't read reliably are left out rather than guessed), plus land-use and zoning hearings (BOCC Land Use, Zoning Hearing Master, Land Use Hearing Officer) from Hillsborough's Legistar. The county's regular agendas are on a site that asks automated tools not to collect it, so full agendas are not here.
