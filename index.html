What your local government decided, in plain words

Free, nonpartisan records of local government meetings in Florida: what was decided, who voted which way, and the official papers, collected every night. It began around Tampa Bay and in southeast Volusia County, and now follows 117 city and county governments across the state.

Everything here comes from official government websites and links back to them. We explain what a decision was. We never say whether it was right.
