What your local government decided, in plain words

Free, nonpartisan records of local government meetings around Tampa Bay and in southeast Volusia County: what was decided, who voted which way, and the official papers, collected every night.

Everything here comes from official government websites and links back to them. We explain what a decision was. We never say whether it was right.
