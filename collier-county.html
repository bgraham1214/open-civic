Collier County: local government records

Collier County · Marco Island

The Collier County government decides zoning, roads and county taxes outside city limits, and county-wide services everywhere. Marco Island has its own city government. City councils and commissions decide zoning, city services and city taxes inside city limits. Other governments in Collier County aren't here yet: see what we cover in Florida.
