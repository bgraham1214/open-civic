Martin County: local government records

Martin County · Jupiter Island · Stuart

The Martin County government decides zoning, roads and county taxes outside city limits, and county-wide services everywhere. Jupiter Island and Stuart each have their own city government. City councils and commissions decide zoning, city services and city taxes inside city limits. Other governments in Martin County aren't here yet: see what we cover in Florida.
