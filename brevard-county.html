Brevard County: local government records

Brevard County · Cocoa · Cocoa Beach · Indian Harbour Beach · Melbourne · Titusville

The Brevard County government decides zoning, roads and county taxes outside city limits, and county-wide services everywhere. Cocoa, Cocoa Beach, Indian Harbour Beach, Melbourne and Titusville each have their own city government. City councils and commissions decide zoning, city services and city taxes inside city limits. Other governments in Brevard County aren't here yet: see what we cover in Florida.
