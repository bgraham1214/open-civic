Palm Beach County: local government records

Delray Beach · Greenacres · Juno Beach · Jupiter · Loxahatchee Groves · Ocean Ridge · Palm Beach · Palm Springs · South Bay · Wellington (Palm Beach County)

Delray Beach, Greenacres, Juno Beach, Jupiter, Loxahatchee Groves, Ocean Ridge, Palm Beach, Palm Springs, South Bay and Wellington each have their own city government. City councils and commissions decide zoning, city services and city taxes inside city limits. Other governments in Palm Beach County aren't here yet: see what we cover in Florida.
