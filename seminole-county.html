Seminole County: local government records

Seminole County · Casselberry · Lake Mary · Longwood

The Seminole County government decides zoning, roads and county taxes outside city limits, and county-wide services everywhere. Casselberry, Lake Mary and Longwood each have their own city government. City councils and commissions decide zoning, city services and city taxes inside city limits. Other governments in Seminole County aren't here yet: see what we cover in Florida.
