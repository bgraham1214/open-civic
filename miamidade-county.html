Miami-Dade County: local government records

Bay Harbor Islands · Coral Gables · Doral · Miami Springs · Opa-locka · Pinecrest (Miami-Dade County)

Bay Harbor Islands, Coral Gables, Doral, Miami Springs, Opa-locka and Pinecrest each have their own city government. City councils and commissions decide zoning, city services and city taxes inside city limits. Other governments in Miami-Dade County aren't here yet: see what we cover in Florida.
