Flagler County: local government records

Flagler County · Flagler Beach

The Flagler County government decides zoning, roads and county taxes outside city limits, and county-wide services everywhere. Flagler Beach has its own city government. City councils and commissions decide zoning, city services and city taxes inside city limits. Other governments in Flagler County aren't here yet: see what we cover in Florida.
