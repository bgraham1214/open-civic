Orange County: local government records

Apopka · Maitland · Ocoee · Winter Garden · Winter Park (Orange County)

Apopka, Maitland, Ocoee, Winter Garden and Winter Park each have their own city government. City councils and commissions decide zoning, city services and city taxes inside city limits. Other governments in Orange County aren't here yet: see what we cover in Florida.
