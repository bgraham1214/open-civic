Polk County: local government records

Polk County · Bartow

The Polk County government decides zoning, roads and county taxes outside city limits, and county-wide services everywhere. Bartow has its own city government. City councils and commissions decide zoning, city services and city taxes inside city limits. Other governments in Polk County aren't here yet: see what we cover in Florida.

What we collect from Polk County

Board of County Commissioners meetings and other public boards, from Polk County's Legistar records, with agenda items, actions and each member's vote where the record gives one, from 2025. Hearings about private residents' own cases (code enforcement, special magistrates, value adjustment boards) are left out. Agendas and minutes are linked on the government's own site.
