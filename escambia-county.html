Escambia County: local government records

Escambia County · Pensacola

The Escambia County government decides zoning, roads and county taxes outside city limits, and county-wide services everywhere. Pensacola has its own city government. City councils and commissions decide zoning, city services and city taxes inside city limits. Other governments in Escambia County aren't here yet: see what we cover in Florida.
