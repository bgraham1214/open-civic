  1. Property tax rates (millage)

    Resolution authorizing the City Manager to advise the Property Appraiser of the City's proposed millage rate, of its rolled-back rate, and the date, time, and place at which a public hearing will be held to consider proposed millage and tentative budget

    What does “Property tax rate (millage)” mean?

    Florida property tax rates are set in “mills.” One mill is $1 of tax for every $1,000 of a property's taxable value. A home with $200,000 of taxable value pays $200 per mill. Source. More
