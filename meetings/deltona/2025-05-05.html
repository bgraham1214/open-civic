    What does “Grant” mean?

    Official wording

    The City of Deltona has been working with the Florida Division of Emergency Management (FDEM) to obtain a grant for the elevation of a portion of Elkcam Boulevard that was flooded during Hurricanes Ian, Nicole and Milton. This impact resulted in Elkcam Boulevard being closed for an extended period of time during all three hurricanes. FDEM requires a resolution that authorizes the City Manager to sign the Federally Funded Subaward and Grant Agreement Number H1135 Project Number 4673-124-R.

    Voted no: Colwell.
