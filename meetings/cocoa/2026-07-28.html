  4. Property tax rates (millage)

    Approve Resolution No. 2026-053, Establishing the Rolled-Back Rate, approve a Proposed Millage Rate for the Purposes of Notification to the Taxpayers by the Brevard County Property Appraiser and set the date, time, and place for the first Public Hearing to consider the FY2027 Budget

    What does “Property tax rate (millage)” mean?

    Florida property tax rates are set in “mills.” One mill is $1 of tax for every $1,000 of a property's taxable value. A home with $200,000 of taxable value pays $200 per mill. Source. More

    Official wording
