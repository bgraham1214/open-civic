    What does “Community Development District” mean?

    A special district a developer can set up under Florida law (Chapter 190) to borrow money for a new community's roads, water and amenities. Homeowners there repay it through an extra yearly charge on their property tax bill. Source. More

  1. Incentive or tax break for a business

    8th & Waldo Economic Development Incentive Grant Program — Action on Two Incomplete Applications (B)

    What does “Incentive or tax break for a business” mean?

    Public money or a tax break offered to a company, usually in exchange for promised jobs or investment. Under Florida law a county can exempt a new or expanding business from part of its property taxes for up to 10 years, once voters have approved that power in a referendum. Cash incentives are paid under an agreement with the company. Source. More
