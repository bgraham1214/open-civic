  7. A public hearing regarding the application for a Major Deviation (PZ-2026-66) to a Major Development Order (Development Orders: DO-21-12, DO-23-16, and DO-25-16) for the Indian Bayou West Subdivision

    Official wording

    A public hearing regarding the application for a Major Deviation (PZ-2026-66) to a Major Development Order (Development Orders: DO-21-12, DO-23-16, and DO-25-16) for the Indian Bayou West Subdivision. The requested deviation seeks approval to remove the proposed connector road identified as Old Creek Trail on the approved plan set. Old Creek Trail is planned to connect Indian Bayou Trail to Commons Drive West, just east of Airport Road. The portion of land designated for the connector road is located within the Indian Bayou Country Club property located at 1 Country Club Drive East (Parcel ID: 00-2S-22-0000-0046-0000). The application requests the removal of Old Creek Trail as a required element of the approved site plan.

    Official record: the meeting agenda.
