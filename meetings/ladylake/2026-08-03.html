    Finance — Discussion and consensus on the tentative millage rate and set proposed dates, times, and place for the first public hearing for the tentative millage rate and budget, and for the second hearing for the final millage rate and budget. (Joella LeDonne)

    What does “Property tax rate (millage)” mean?

    Florida property tax rates are set in “mills.” One mill is $1 of tax for every $1,000 of a property's taxable value. A home with $200,000 of taxable value pays $200 per mill. Source. More
