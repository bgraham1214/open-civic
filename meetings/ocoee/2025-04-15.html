  1. Change to the future land-use map

    First Reading Transmittal and Public Hearing of an Ordinance for Envision 2045 Ocoee Comprehensive Plan - Large Scale Plan Amendment to Replace Ocoee's Comprehensive Plan in its Entirety. (Zoning Manager Whitfield)

    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The city's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. More

  2. Change to the city's development rules

    Approval to Terminate Contract #23-010 with Kimley-Horn & Associates to Update the City's Land Development Code. (Development Services Director Ginger)

    What does “Change to the development rules” mean?

    The Land Development Code is the city's rulebook for building: lot sizes, setbacks, parking, signs and so on. This changes the rulebook itself, for everyone it applies to. More
