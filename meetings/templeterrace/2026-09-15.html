    Public Hearing and First Reading of an Ordinance Approving Comprehensive Plan Future Land Use Amendment, Case CPA-26-01 for Folio No. 061363-0100 from Hillsborough County Community Mixed Use-12 (CMU-12) to Temple Terrace Community Mixed Use-12 (CMU-12)

    • Land-use change: from Community Mixed Use (up to 12 homes per acre) to Community Mixed Use (up to 12 homes per acre)
    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The city's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. More
