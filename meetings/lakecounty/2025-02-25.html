    Annual Review for Economic Development Incentives Compliance

    What does “Incentive or tax break for a business” mean?

    Public money or a tax break offered to a company, usually in exchange for promised jobs or investment. Under Florida law a county can exempt a new or expanding business from part of its property taxes for up to 10 years, once voters have approved that power in a referendum.
