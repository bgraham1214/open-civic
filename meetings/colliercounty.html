The Collier County Commission makes county-wide decisions, and the local decisions for everyone who lives outside a city. Pick a meeting to see what was on its agenda, explained without the jargon.

Collier County records its votes in its minutes, which we do not read yet, so these pages show agendas, not outcomes.
