    What does “Community Development District” mean?

    A special district a developer can set up under Florida law (Chapter 190) to borrow money for a new community's roads, water and amenities. Homeowners there repay it through an extra yearly charge on their property tax bill. Source. More

Money (1)

  1. Property tax rates (millage)

    Acknowledge receipt of the FY 2025-2026 Parrish Medical Center Revenue and Expense Budget and Millage Resolution

    What does “Property tax rate (millage)” mean?

    Florida property tax rates are set in “mills.” One mill is $1 of tax for every $1,000 of a property's taxable value. A home with $200,000 of taxable value pays $200 per mill. Source. More
