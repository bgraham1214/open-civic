Building and land use (2)

New to this? How building and land decisions work

  1. Change to the future land-use map at 42501 U

    Ordinance 2024-06: Future Land Use Map Amendment for property located at 42501 U.S. Highway 19 North (Application 22-02); Legislative (2nd Reading) (Renea Vincent Planning and Zoning Director)

    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The city's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. More

    Official wording

    Ordinance 2024-06: Future Land Use Map Amendment for property located at 42501 U.S. Highway 19 North (Application 22-02); Legislative (2nd Reading) (Renea Vincent Planning and Zoning Director)

    Official record: the meeting agenda.

  2. Change to the city's development rules

    Ordinance 2024-01; Land Development Code Amendment: Amending Article VII, Heritage Preservation, and, Providing for Creation of Neighborhood Conservation Overlay Districts (Application 22-112) Legislative (1st Reading) This item will be heard at, or after, 7:30 p.m. (Renea Vincent Planning and Zoning Director)

    What does “Change to the development rules” mean?

    The Land Development Code is the city's rulebook for building: lot sizes, setbacks, parking, signs and so on. This changes the rulebook itself, for everyone it applies to. More

    Official wording
