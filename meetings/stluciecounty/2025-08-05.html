  1. Change to the future land-use map

    Adoption Hearing - P1 Motor Club - Proposed Comprehensive Plan Future Land Use Map Amendment to Revise P1 Motor Club Special District Regulatory Standard # 1

    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The county's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. Source. More

  2. Community Development District (special taxing district for a new community)

    Ordinance - Establishing the Pineapple Grove Community Development District - Permission to Advertise

    What does “Community Development District” mean?

    A special district a developer can set up under Florida law (Chapter 190) to borrow money for a new community's roads, water and amenities. Homeowners there repay it through an extra yearly charge on their property tax bill. Source. More

    Official wording

    Ordinance - Establishing the Pineapple Grove Community Development District - Permission to Advertise

    Official record: the meeting agenda.

  3. Change to the county's development rules

    Permission to Advertise - Land Development Code Text Amendment to Chapter XIII Building Regulations and Public Works Construction Manual, Creating Milestone Inspection Guidelines

    What does “Change to the development rules” mean?

    The Land Development Code is the county's rulebook for building: lot sizes, setbacks, parking, signs and so on. This changes the rulebook itself, for everyone it applies to. Source. More

  2. Property tax rates (millage)

    As we enter the peak of hurricane season, residents are reminded that now is the time to trim trees and vegetation in their yards

    What does “Property tax rate (millage)” mean?

    Florida property tax rates are set in “mills.” One mill is $1 of tax for every $1,000 of a property's taxable value. A home with $200,000 of taxable value pays $200 per mill. Source. More

    Official wording

    As we enter the peak of hurricane season, residents are reminded that now is the time to trim trees and vegetation in their yards. Please do not wait until a storm is threatening our area. Residents who may need emergency sheltering during a storm are encouraged now to pre-register for our pet-friendly and medical special needs shelters. Those forms and other hurricane preparedness tips can be found at www.readystlucie.org.

    Official record: the meeting agenda.
