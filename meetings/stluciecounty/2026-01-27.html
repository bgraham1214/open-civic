    Official wording

    A reminder that St. Lucie County’s Aquatics staff hosts Aqua Fit classes at the Lakewood Park Pool every Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday from 10 to 11 a.m. Admission is $5 per class. Designed for adults, aquafit classes are low-impact workouts conducted in the shallow, zero-entry, temperature-controlled pool and are designed to improve muscle strength, joint stability, coordination and heart conditioning. The Lakewood Park Pool is located at 5990 Emerson Ave in Fort Pierce.

  9. Involves money: $5

    St. Lucie County’s Parks & Recreation staff is offering a new Tiny Tots Track & Field program every Tuesday and Thursday from 10:30 to 11:30 a.m. at Lawnwood Stadium throughout February

    Official wording

    St. Lucie County’s Parks & Recreation staff is offering a new Tiny Tots Track & Field program every Tuesday and Thursday from 10:30 to 11:30 a.m. at Lawnwood Stadium throughout February. Designed for ages six and younger, activities include relay races, beanbag and ball tosses, hula hoop jumps, storytime and more. Parents are required to stay on-site during the activities. The cost is $5 per student per session. Pre-registration and payment are required by visiting www.stlucieco.gov/parks.

    Official record: the meeting agenda.
