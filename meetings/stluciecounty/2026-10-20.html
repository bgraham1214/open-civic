  30. The 6th Annual Culinary Cook-off Competition returns to the Sunshine Kitchen on Tuesday, Oct. 27

    Official wording

    The 6th Annual Culinary Cook-off Competition returns to the Sunshine Kitchen on Tuesday, Oct. 27. In partnership with Indian River State College and St. Lucie County Public Schools, the Sunshine Kitchen hosts this annual event where five St. Lucie County high school students and five Indian River State College students compete against each other in a culinary challenge. The competition prep work begins at 9:50 a.m., while cooking starts at 10:05 a.m. Judging will take place at 11:05 a.m. and awards will be presented at noon. The public is invited to attend and enjoy this free event. Those interested in attending should make reservations by emailing Regina.McCants@stlucieco.gov before Friday, Oct. 16. Refreshments will be provided by students enrolled in culinary programs through the St. Lucie Public Schools District.

    Official record: the meeting agenda.
