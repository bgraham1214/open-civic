    What does “Contract or purchase” mean?

    The city agrees to pay a company or group for goods or work. ITB (invitation to bid) and RFP (request for proposals) are the ways the city asks companies to compete for the job. More

    Official wording

    Authorize staff to negotiate and award a contract with Club Car LLC for the Golf Car Replacement (CB26-009), to include a four-year lease/purchase agreement with DLL Finance LLC. The agreement would include applying the balance of the current golf carts trade-in value, not to exceed $592,574.40. In the event an agreement cannot be reached with Club Car LLC, staff is authorized to proceed with negotiations with the remaining ranked firm(s), including the option to re-solicit proposals through a new RFP if necessary, until a contract is executed.
