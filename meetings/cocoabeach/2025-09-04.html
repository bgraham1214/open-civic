  1. Property tax rates (millage)

    Approve the Tentative Millage rate of 6.0000 mills for Fiscal Year 2026

    What does “Property tax rate (millage)” mean?

    Florida property tax rates are set in “mills.” One mill is $1 of tax for every $1,000 of a property's taxable value. A home with $200,000 of taxable value pays $200 per mill. Source. More

    Official wording

    Approve the Tentative Millage rate of 6.0000 mills for Fiscal Year 2026. The Proposed Millage rate of 6.0000 mills is 0.65% higher than the Rollback rate of 5.9610 mills.
