    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The city's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. More

    Official wording

    Sperry Parcel – Applications for 10.5± acres of property located at 961 and 997 N A1A, north of N A1A and east of Clemons Street. A. Ordinance 31-26, First Reading, Future Land Use Map (FLUM) amendment to change the future land use designation of 1.8± acres of Riverwalk Flex and 8.7± acres of High Density Residential to 5.0± acres of Inlet Village Flex and 5.5± acres of Conservation, and to reduce the Riverwalk Corridor component from 535 to 142 linear feet. B. Ordinance 32-26, First Reading, Zoning Map amendment to assign Inlet Village Zoning District - Flex South Subdistrict to 5.0± acres; Conservation Preservation to 4.1± acres; and Conservation Restricted to 1.4± acres.
