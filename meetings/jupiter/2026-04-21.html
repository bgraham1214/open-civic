    Jupiter Christian School Legacy Campus (fka Hawkeye)– A. Ordinance 21-26, First Reading — Future Land Use Map amendment from General Industrial with Bioscience Research Protection Overlay to High Density Residential, on 18.0± acres generally located east of I-95, north of Jupiter Park Drive, and 180± feet west of…

    • Land-use change: from Industrial, Park, Light Industry (up to 1 home per acre) to Residential, Limited Multifamily (up to 3 homes per acre)
    • Size: about 18.0 acres
    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The city's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. More
