    (Legislative) LAND DEVELOPMENT CODE TEXT AMENDMENT - ORDINANCE 2026-04 AMENDING LAND DEVELOPMENT CODE SECTION 5.03.00 SIGNS TO PERMIT LIMITED SIGNAGE ON CONVERSATION LANDS FOR THE RECOGNITION OF DONORS, SPONSORS, OR PARTNERS WHO HAVE CONTRIBUTED TO THE ACQUISITION, RESTORATION, OR STEWARDSHIP OF SUCH PROPERTIES, WHILE PRESERVING THE NATURAL CHARACTER AND ECOLOGICAL INTEGRITY OF THESE LANDS; AUTHORIZING ADMINISTRATIVE CORRECTION OF SCRIVENER’S ERRORS; PROVIDING FOR SEVERABILITY; AND PROVIDING FOR AN EFFECTIVE DATE. Synopsis: Amends the Land Development Code (LDC) to establish regulations and design criteria for conservation signage. Currently, the ordinance prohibits signs on properties designated as Conservation (CON) zones. The suggested ordinance aims to allow certain signage on conservation lands which acknowledges donors, sponsors, or partners who have played a role in the acquisition, restoration, or management of these areas, all while maintaining the natural character and ecological integrity of the land. The language in red in Exhibit A reflects a change recommended by the Planning Advisory Board in 5.05.02(1) to clarify the reference to five (5) acres and changes recommended by City Attorney in 5.05.02(6) to address the requirements of the permit.
