    Change to the county's development rules

    LDC 17-0331 - Medical Marijuana Dispensary - The proposed amendment to the LDC regarding medical marijuana dispensing facilities amended the LDC to establish zoning & land development regulations related to the location

    What does “Change to the development rules” mean?

    The Land Development Code is the county's rulebook for building: lot sizes, setbacks, parking, signs and so on. This changes the rulebook itself, for everyone it applies to. Source. More

    Official wording

    LDC 17-0331 - Medical Marijuana Dispensary - The proposed amendment to the LDC regarding medical marijuana dispensing facilities amended the LDC to establish zoning & land development regulations related to the location... Moved the Board extend the moratorium to 10/1/2017.

    Voted no: Ken Hagan, Pat Kemp, Stacy White, Victor Crist.
