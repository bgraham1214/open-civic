Building and land use (2)

New to this? How building and land decisions work

  1. Change to the future land-use map

    HC/CPA 18-01 - Future Land Use Map Change – County Road 579, North of Interstate 4 – Proposed Change on 19.20 Acres, More or Less, From Residential 4 to Light Industrial Planned

    • Size: about 19.20 acres
    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The county's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. Source. More

    Official wording

    HC/CPA 18-01 - Future Land Use Map Change – County Road 579, North of Interstate 4 – Proposed Change on 19.20 Acres, More or Less, From Residential 4 to Light Industrial Planned. Moved approval.

    Passed 7 to 0.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2018 voting record, page 45. Look for roll call (RCS) 590.
