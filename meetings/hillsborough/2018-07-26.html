  1. Budget or funding decision: $500,000 (largest amount named)

    Budget Recon

    What does “Budget change” mean?

    Moves money within the county budget or adds money that came in after the budget was set. More

    Official wording

    Budget Recon. Amendment to the motion that the $500,000 would come out of the HART allocation of $2.6 mill because the Board would not be able to spend all that by the time the Board was done w/ all items the Board had for HART; so it would be $2.1 mill

    Passed 4 to 3.
