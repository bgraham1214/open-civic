    HC/CPA 17-04 - FLUM Change - Lithia Pinecrest Rd., North of Browning Rd. - Change on 164 acres from Agricultural Rural-1/5 to Residential 9

    • Size: about 164 acres
    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The county's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. Source. More

    Official wording

    HC/CPA 17-04 - FLUM Change - Lithia Pinecrest Rd., North of Browning Rd. - Change on 164 acres from Agricultural Rural-1/5 to Residential 9. Moved to continue to October 26, 2017.

    Passed 4 to 0.

    Didn't vote: Lesley Miller, Sandra Murman, Victor Crist.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2017 voting record, page 52. Look for roll call (RCS) 902.
