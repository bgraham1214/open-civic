  3. Change to the Urban Service Area (where the county plans water and sewer)

    HC/CPA 26-05 Comprehensive Plan Map Amendment - Proposed change on 190± acres to expand the Urban Service Area Boundary Location: 18308 Dorman Road

    • Size: about 190 acres
    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The county's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. Source. More

    What does “Urban Service Area” mean?

    The boundary where the county plans to provide city-style services like water and sewer. The county aims to put 80% of new growth inside it. Moving the line lets land outside it ask for more homes or businesses. Source. More

    Official wording

    HC/CPA 26-05 Comprehensive Plan Map Amendment - Proposed change on 190± acres to expand the Urban Service Area Boundary Location: 18308 Dorman Road. Chair Hagan recommended two minutes for each speaker. Moved to approve.

    Passed 7 to 0.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2026 voting record, page 32. Look for roll call (RCS) 5521.
