  1. Property tax rates (millage)

    What does “Property tax rate (millage)” mean?

    Florida property tax rates are set in “mills.” One mill is $1 of tax for every $1,000 of a property's taxable value. A home with $200,000 of taxable value pays $200 per mill. Source. More

    Official wording

    Oral Vote. PH/Budget, Adoption of Tentative Millage Rates Resolution for FY 2021. So moved.

    Passed 7 to 0.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2020 voting record, page 105. Look for roll call (RCS) 2247.
