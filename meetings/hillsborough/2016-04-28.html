New to this? How building and land decisions work

  1. Change to the future land-use map at Hillsborough Ave, E of Race Track Rd

    CPA 15-13 - Future Land Use Map Change - Hillsborough Ave, E of Race Track Rd - Proposed designation change on 3.44 acres from light industrial to regional mixed use 35 (small scale amendment)

    • Size: about 3.44 acres
    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The county's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. Source. More

    Official wording

    CPA 15-13 - Future Land Use Map Change - Hillsborough Ave, E of Race Track Rd - Proposed designation change on 3.44 acres from light industrial to regional mixed use 35 (small scale amendment). Moved to continue the item to the CPA public hearing on May 26, 2016, at 6:00 p.m.

    Passed 5 to 0.

    Didn't vote: Al Higginbotham, Stacy White.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2016 voting record, page 22. Look for roll call (RCS) 913.
