Hillsborough County Commission meeting

Building and land use (6)

New to this? How building and land decisions work

  1. Change to the county's development rules

    LDC 15-1268 - Adequate Public Facilities

    What does “Change to the development rules” mean?

    The Land Development Code is the county's rulebook for building: lot sizes, setbacks, parking, signs and so on. This changes the rulebook itself, for everyone it applies to. Source. More

    Official wording

    LDC 15-1268 - Adequate Public Facilities. LDC 15-1269 - Enforcing Authorities. LDC 15-1270 - Appeals for Land Use Hearing Officer to Land Use Appeals Board. Moved to adopt Items I.1., I.2., and I.3. & the ordinance. Ordinance 15-32

    Passed 6 to 0.

    Didn't vote: Victor Crist.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2015 voting record, page 64. Look for roll call (RCS) 683.
