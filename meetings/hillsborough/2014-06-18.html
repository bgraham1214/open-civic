Building and land use (1)

New to this? How building and land decisions work

  1. Change to the county's development rules

    Endorse submittal of amendments to LDC related to concurrent site development & building permit review & self-certification of final landscaping & lot grading inspections to be processed as part of 2014 Round 2 amendments

    What does “Change to the development rules” mean?

    The Land Development Code is the county's rulebook for building: lot sizes, setbacks, parking, signs and so on. This changes the rulebook itself, for everyone it applies to. Source. More

    Official wording

    Endorse submittal of amendments to LDC related to concurrent site development & building permit review & self-certification of final landscaping & lot grading inspections to be processed as part of 2014 Round 2 amendments. Moved to accept the recommendation.

    Passed 7 to 0.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2014 voting record, page 35. Look for roll call (RCS) 421.

Local laws (1)

  1. New restriction

    County law on synthetic drugs

    Conduct a public hearing to enact an ordinance amending Section 36-507 of Hillsborough County Code of Ordinances and Laws, Chapter 36, Article VII, relating to the possession, sale, or manufacture of synthetic drugs in the County

    What does “Ordinance” mean?

    A local law. Once adopted it applies to everyone in the county area it covers. More

    Official wording
