    BOCC Discussion of Potential Changes to the Millage and/or Budget

    What does “Property tax rate (millage)” mean?

    Florida property tax rates are set in “mills.” One mill is $1 of tax for every $1,000 of a property's taxable value. A home with $200,000 of taxable value pays $200 per mill. Source. More

    Official wording

    BOCC Discussion of Potential Changes to the Millage and/or Budget. Made a substitute motion leaving $2 million and taking $8 million and redirecting.

    Failed 4 to 3.

    Voted no: Donna Cameron Cepeda, Joshua Wostal, Ken Hagan, Michael Owen.
