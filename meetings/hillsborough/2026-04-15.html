Building and land use (1)

New to this? How building and land decisions work

  1. Urban Service Area (where the county plans water and sewer)

    Presentation and briefing by County-City Planning Commission regarding concerns arising in Hillsborough County in the 33 years since the 1993 initiation of the Urban Service Area

    What does “Urban Service Area” mean?

    The boundary where the county plans to provide city-style services like water and sewer. The county aims to put 80% of new growth inside it. Moving the line lets land outside it ask for more homes or businesses. Source. More

    Official wording

    Presentation and briefing by County-City Planning Commission regarding concerns arising in Hillsborough County in the 33 years since the 1993 initiation of the Urban Service Area. Moved to receive the report.

    Passed 7 to 0.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2026 voting record, page 20. Look for roll call (RCS) 5424.
