New to this? How building and land decisions work

  1. Change to the Urban Service Area (where the county plans water and sewer)

    HC/CPA 24-07 Comprehensive Plan Text Amendment – 5711 Bishop Road Urban Service Area Extension

    What does “Change to the wording of the long-range plan” mean?

    This changes the written policies of the county's long-range land plan, which apply across many properties rather than one. Source. More

    What does “Urban Service Area” mean?

    The boundary where the county plans to provide city-style services like water and sewer. The county aims to put 80% of new growth inside it. Moving the line lets land outside it ask for more homes or businesses. Source. More

    Official wording

    HC/CPA 24-07 Comprehensive Plan Text Amendment – 5711 Bishop Road Urban Service Area Extension. Moved for approval.

    Passed 6 to 1.

    Voted no: Donna Cameron Cepeda.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2025 voting record, page 8. Look for roll call (RCS) 4753.
