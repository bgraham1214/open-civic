  1. Community Development District (special taxing district for a new community)

    CDD 16-0670 - Petition to expand the boundaries of Bullfrog Creek CDD - Approve the expansion of the Bullfrog CDD in accordance w/ the ordinance

    What does “Community Development District” mean?

    A special district a developer can set up under Florida law (Chapter 190) to borrow money for a new community's roads, water and amenities. Homeowners there repay it through an extra yearly charge on their property tax bill. Source. More

    Official wording

    CDD 16-0670 - Petition to expand the boundaries of Bullfrog Creek CDD - Approve the expansion of the Bullfrog CDD in accordance w/ the ordinance. Moved approval. Ordinance 16-15.

    Passed 6 to 0.

    Didn't vote: Ken Hagan.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2016 voting record, page 40. Look for roll call (RCS) 37.
