  1. Community Development District (special taxing district for a new community)

    Community Development District (CDD) 15-0868 - Petition to establish Ventana CDD - Establish the Ventana CDD in accordance w/the ordinance

    What does “Community Development District” mean?

    A special district a developer can set up under Florida law (Chapter 190) to borrow money for a new community's roads, water and amenities. Homeowners there repay it through an extra yearly charge on their property tax bill. Source. More

    Official wording

    Community Development District (CDD) 15-0868 - Petition to establish Ventana CDD - Establish the Ventana CDD in accordance w/the ordinance. Moved approval. Ordinance 16-6

    Passed 6 to 0.

    Didn't vote: Ken Hagan.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2016 voting record, page 19. Look for roll call (RCS) 883.
