Hillsborough County Commission meeting

The Hillsborough County Commission voted on 12 decisions: 1 on money, 1 on local laws and 10 others. Every recorded vote was unanimous.

Money (1)

  1. Grant

    Approve first modification to the Community Development Block Grant funding agreement with Tampa Housing Authority and Affordable Housing Services for rehabilitation of Palm Terrace Apartments

    What does “Grant” mean?

    Money given for a specific purpose, either to the county (often from the state or federal government) or from the county to a local group. More

    Official wording

    Approve first modification to the Community Development Block Grant funding agreement with Tampa Housing Authority and Affordable Housing Services for rehabilitation of Palm Terrace Apartments. Moved the item.

    Passed 6 to 0.

    Didn't vote: Stacy White.
