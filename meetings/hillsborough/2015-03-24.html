  1. Community Development District (special taxing district for a new community)

    CDD 15-0059 - Petition to expand the Wynnmere West CDD - Approve the expansion of the Wynnmere West CDD in accordance w/ the ordinance

    What does “Community Development District” mean?

    A special district a developer can set up under Florida law (Chapter 190) to borrow money for a new community's roads, water and amenities. Homeowners there repay it through an extra yearly charge on their property tax bill. Source. More

    Official wording

    CDD 15-0059 - Petition to expand the Wynnmere West CDD - Approve the expansion of the Wynnmere West CDD in accordance w/ the ordinance. Moved approval. Ordinance 15-5.

    Passed 7 to 0.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2015 voting record, page 16. Look for roll call (RCS) 209.

  5. Change to the future land-use map

    Comprehensive Plan Amendment (CPA), Adoption Public Hearing for CPA 14-08

    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The county's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. Source. More

    Official wording

    Comprehensive Plan Amendment (CPA), Adoption Public Hearing for CPA 14-08. Moved approval. Ordinance 15-4.

    Passed 7 to 0.
