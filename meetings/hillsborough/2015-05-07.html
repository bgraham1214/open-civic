New to this? How building and land decisions work

  1. Change to the future land-use map

    CPA 14-10-6-M - Future land use map change - Columbus Drive - Proposed change from Office Commerical 20 to Light Industrial on 124 +/- acres

    • Size: about 124 acres
    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The county's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. Source. More

    Official wording

    CPA 14-10-6-M - Future land use map change - Columbus Drive - Proposed change from Office Commerical 20 to Light Industrial on 124 +/- acres. Moved to move The Buttrey Property LLC parcel out & keep the rest intact.

    Passed 7 to 0.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2015 voting record, page 25. Look for roll call (RCS) 288.
