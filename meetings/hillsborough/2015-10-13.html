New to this? How building and land decisions work

  1. Community Development District (special taxing district for a new community)

    CDD 15-0867 - Petition to establish South Fork III CDD in accordance w/ordinance in background material

    What does “Community Development District” mean?

    A special district a developer can set up under Florida law (Chapter 190) to borrow money for a new community's roads, water and amenities. Homeowners there repay it through an extra yearly charge on their property tax bill. Source. More

    Official wording

    CDD 15-0867 - Petition to establish South Fork III CDD in accordance w/ordinance in background material. Moved approval. (Ordinance 15-22)

    Passed 7 to 0.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2015 voting record, page 54. Look for roll call (RCS) 579.

  2. Change to the county's development rules

    LDC 15-1268, LDC 15-1269, and 15-1270 - Moved to incorporate the LDC amendments into the November 10 and December 8, 2015, land use meetings

    What does “Change to the development rules” mean?

    The Land Development Code is the county's rulebook for building: lot sizes, setbacks, parking, signs and so on. This changes the rulebook itself, for everyone it applies to. Source. More

    Official wording

    LDC 15-1268, LDC 15-1269, and 15-1270 - Moved to incorporate the LDC amendments into the November 10 and December 8, 2015, land use meetings.

    Passed 7 to 0.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2015 voting record, page 55. Look for roll call (RCS) 590.
