New to this? How building and land decisions work

  1. Change to the future land-use map

    HC/CPA 17-02 - FLUE Text Change - FLUE Industrial Land Use Categories Increase in the FAR - Increasing the FAR from 0.50 to 0.75

    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The county's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. Source. More

    Official wording

    HC/CPA 17-02 - FLUE Text Change - FLUE Industrial Land Use Categories Increase in the FAR - Increasing the FAR from 0.50 to 0.75. Moved approval.

    Passed 6 to 0.

    Didn't vote: Al Higginbotham.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2017 voting record, page 84. Look for roll call (RCS) 217.

  2. Change to the Urban Service Area (where the county plans water and sewer)

    HC/CPA 17-07 - FLUE Text Change - FLUE Policy 1.9 Revision - Proposed revision to modify criteria for parcels split by the Urban Service Area

    What does “Change to the wording of the long-range plan” mean?

    This changes the written policies of the county's long-range land plan, which apply across many properties rather than one. Source. More

    What does “Urban Service Area” mean?

    The boundary where the county plans to provide city-style services like water and sewer. The county aims to put 80% of new growth inside it. Moving the line lets land outside it ask for more homes or businesses. Source. More

    Official wording

    HC/CPA 17-07 - FLUE Text Change - FLUE Policy 1.9 Revision - Proposed revision to modify criteria for parcels split by the Urban Service Area. Substitute motion the Board deny HC/CPA 17-07. FAILED

    Failed 4 to 2.

    Voted no: Pat Kemp, Sandra Murman, Stacy White, Victor Crist. Didn't vote: Al Higginbotham.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2017 voting record, page 84. Look for roll call (RCS) 218.

    What does “Change to the development rules” mean?

    The Land Development Code is the county's rulebook for building: lot sizes, setbacks, parking, signs and so on. This changes the rulebook itself, for everyone it applies to. Source. More
