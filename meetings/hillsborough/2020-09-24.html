Building and land use (7)

New to this? How building and land decisions work

  1. Change to the county's development rules

    Oral Vote

    What does “Change to the development rules” mean?

    The Land Development Code is the county's rulebook for building: lot sizes, setbacks, parking, signs and so on. This changes the rulebook itself, for everyone it applies to. Source. More

    Official wording

    Oral Vote. LDC 19-1055 Accessory Dwellings - The purpose of this proposed amendment is to clarify and modify the standards of the Accessory Dwelling provision of the Land Development Code. Moved approval.

    Passed 7 to 0.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2020 voting record, page 113. Look for roll call (RCS) 2275.

  2. Urban Service Area (where the county plans water and sewer)

    Oral Vote

    What does “Urban Service Area” mean?

    The boundary where the county plans to provide city-style services like water and sewer. The county aims to put 80% of new growth inside it. Moving the line lets land outside it ask for more homes or businesses. Source. More

    Official wording

    Oral Vote. LDC 20-0720 Wastewater Connection to a publicly owned and operated wastewater facility for the subdivision of a Parent Parcel or Lot of Record in the Urban Service Area. Moved approval.

    Passed 7 to 0.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2020 voting record, page 113. Look for roll call (RCS) 2276.
