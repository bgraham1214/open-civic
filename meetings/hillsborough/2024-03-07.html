New to this? How building and land decisions work

  1. Change to the future land-use map at Future Land Use Map Amendment: located northwest of Symmes Road and East Bay Road. Propose

    HC/CPA 22-18 - Future Land Use Map Amendment: located northwest of Symmes Road and East Bay Road

    • Size: about 8.40 acres
    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The county's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. Source. More

    Official wording

    HC/CPA 22-18 - Future Land Use Map Amendment: located northwest of Symmes Road and East Bay Road. Proposed change on ±8.40 acres from Residential-6 and Residential-9 to Residential-20. Moved to deny the increase to Res-20.

    Passed 5 to 2.

    Voted no: Harry Cohen, Pat Kemp.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2024 voting record, page 11. Look for roll call (RCS) 4250.
