  3. Property tax rates (millage)

    Direct the County Administrator to prepare for consideration at the final budget public hearing on September 17, 2015, a proposed Countywide General Fund millage decrease of 0.0017 mills

    What does “Property tax rate (millage)” mean?

    Florida property tax rates are set in “mills.” One mill is $1 of tax for every $1,000 of a property's taxable value. A home with $200,000 of taxable value pays $200 per mill. Source. More

    Official wording

    Direct the County Administrator to prepare for consideration at the final budget public hearing on September 17, 2015, a proposed Countywide General Fund millage decrease of 0.0017 mills. Moved to waive the time certain.

    Passed 7 to 0.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2015 voting record, page 47. Look for roll call (RCS) 508.
