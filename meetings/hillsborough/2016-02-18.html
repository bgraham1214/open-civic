Building and land use (1)

New to this? How building and land decisions work

  1. Change to the future land-use map

    CPA 15-06 - Future Land Use Map Change - Veterans Expressway and Gunn Highway - Proposed change from RES 4 to RES 20 on 14.14 acres

    • Size: about 14.14 acres
    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The county's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. Source. More

    Official wording

    CPA 15-06 - Future Land Use Map Change - Veterans Expressway and Gunn Highway - Proposed change from RES 4 to RES 20 on 14.14 acres. Moved approval.

    Passed 5 to 0.

    Didn't vote: Al Higginbotham, Stacy White.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2016 voting record, page 11. Look for roll call (RCS) 812.
