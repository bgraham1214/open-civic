Building and land use (3)

New to this? How building and land decisions work

  1. Change to the county's development rules

    Adoption of ordinance and announcement of effective dates for LDC 14-0471, LDC 14-0472, and LDC 14-0474

    What does “Change to the development rules” mean?

    The Land Development Code is the county's rulebook for building: lot sizes, setbacks, parking, signs and so on. This changes the rulebook itself, for everyone it applies to. Source. More

    Official wording

    Adoption of ordinance and announcement of effective dates for LDC 14-0471, LDC 14-0472, and LDC 14-0474. Moved approval. (Ordinance 14-18)

    Passed 7 to 0.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2014 voting record, page 33. Look for roll call (RCS) 397.

  2. Change to the future land-use map at U.S. Highway 301 and Ohio Avenue

    Future Land Use Map Change - U.S. Highway 301 and Ohio Avenue - Proposed change on 14.12 acres from the Res-4 to the Suburban Mixed Use 6 plan designation

    • Size: about 14.12 acres
    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The county's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. Source. More

    Official wording

    Future Land Use Map Change - U.S. Highway 301 and Ohio Avenue - Proposed change on 14.12 acres from the Res-4 to the Suburban Mixed Use 6 plan designation. Moved denial of the item.

    Passed 7 to 0.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2014 voting record, page 33. Look for roll call (RCS) 399.
