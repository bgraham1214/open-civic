  2. Change to the Urban Service Area (where the county plans water and sewer)

    HC/CPA 25-04 Comprehensive Plan Map Amendment – Lutz Lake Fern Road - Proposal to expand the Urban Service Area boundary to incorporate 295.6 acres, more or less

    • Size: about 295.6 acres
    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The county's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. Source. More

    What does “Urban Service Area” mean?

    The boundary where the county plans to provide city-style services like water and sewer. The county aims to put 80% of new growth inside it. Moving the line lets land outside it ask for more homes or businesses. Source. More

    Official wording

    HC/CPA 25-04 Comprehensive Plan Map Amendment – Lutz Lake Fern Road - Proposal to expand the Urban Service Area boundary to incorporate 295.6 acres, more or less. So moved for each individual public comment speaker to be given two minutes.

    Passed 6 to 0.

    Didn't vote: Joshua Wostal.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2026 voting record, page 14. Look for roll call (RCS) 5378.
