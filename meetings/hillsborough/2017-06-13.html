  1. Community Development District (special taxing district for a new community)

    CDD 17-0524 Petition to establish Boyette Park CDD in accordance w/ the ordinance

    • Size: about 108 acres
    What does “Community Development District” mean?

    A special district a developer can set up under Florida law (Chapter 190) to borrow money for a new community's roads, water and amenities. Homeowners there repay it through an extra yearly charge on their property tax bill. Source. More

    Official wording

    CDD 17-0524 Petition to establish Boyette Park CDD in accordance w/ the ordinance. No direct financial impact to the County would occur as a result of the petition. The Boyette Park CDD would encompass, more ore less, 108 acres. Moved approval.

    Passed 6 to 0.

    Didn't vote: Stacy White.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2017 voting record, page 36. Look for roll call (RCS) 744.
