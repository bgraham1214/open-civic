  1. Urban Service Area (where the county plans water and sewer)

    LDC 25-0957 – Subidivsion of Parcels on Septic Tank in the Urban Service Area - This proposed amendment to Section 4.03.02 of the Land Development Code (LDC) will revise the requirements for sanitary sewer connections for development

    What does “Urban Service Area” mean?

    The boundary where the county plans to provide city-style services like water and sewer. The county aims to put 80% of new growth inside it. Moving the line lets land outside it ask for more homes or businesses. Source. More
