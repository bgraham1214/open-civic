  1. Community Development District (special taxing district for a new community)

    CDD 15-0690 Petition to Contract the Boundaries of the Fishhawk II CDD - Approve the contraction of the Fishhawk II CDD boundaries by 2.76 acres in accordance with the ordinance

    • Size: about 2.76 acres
    What does “Community Development District” mean?

    A special district a developer can set up under Florida law (Chapter 190) to borrow money for a new community's roads, water and amenities. Homeowners there repay it through an extra yearly charge on their property tax bill. Source. More

    Official wording

    CDD 15-0690 Petition to Contract the Boundaries of the Fishhawk II CDD - Approve the contraction of the Fishhawk II CDD boundaries by 2.76 acres in accordance with the ordinance. Moved approval. (Ordinance 15-13)

    Passed 7 to 0.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2015 voting record, page 30. Look for roll call (RCS) 342.
