New to this? How building and land decisions work

  1. Change to the Urban Service Area (where the county plans water and sewer)

    HC/CPA 24-09 Comprehensive Plan Text Amendment – Saffold Road and CR 579 Urban Service Area Extension

    What does “Change to the wording of the long-range plan” mean?

    This changes the written policies of the county's long-range land plan, which apply across many properties rather than one. Source. More

    What does “Urban Service Area” mean?

    The boundary where the county plans to provide city-style services like water and sewer. The county aims to put 80% of new growth inside it. Moving the line lets land outside it ask for more homes or businesses. Source. More

    Official wording

    HC/CPA 24-09 Comprehensive Plan Text Amendment – Saffold Road and CR 579 Urban Service Area Extension. Moved for approval.

    Passed 5 to 0.

    Didn't vote: Donna Cameron Cepeda, Gwen Myers.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2025 voting record, page 2. Look for roll call (RCS) 4706.

  2. Change to the future land-use map

    HC/CPA 24-08 Comprehensive Plan Map Amendment – Proposed change on 638.77 acres, more or less, from Wimauma Village Residential-2 (WVR-2) to Residential-4 (RES-4) - Saffold Road & CR 579

    • Land-use change: from Wimauma Village Residential (up to 2 homes per acre) to Residential (up to 4 homes per acre)
    • Size: about 638.77 acres
    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The county's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. Source. More
