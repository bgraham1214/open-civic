    • Size: about 1.66 acres
    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The county's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. Source. More

    Official wording

    HC/CPA 23-13 - Future Land Use Map Amendment: Located at 5223 CR 579, east of Mango Road and north of Clay Pitt Road. Proposed change on 1.66 acres, more or less, from Residential-4 to Residential- 9. Moved approval.

    Passed 7 to 0.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2023 voting record, page 52. Look for roll call (RCS) 4070.
