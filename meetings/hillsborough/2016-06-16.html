Building and land use (1)

New to this? How building and land decisions work

  1. Change to the county's development rules

    LDC 16-0595 - Proposed text amendment to delete the references to transportation concurrency management from the LDC

    What does “Change to the development rules” mean?

    The Land Development Code is the county's rulebook for building: lot sizes, setbacks, parking, signs and so on. This changes the rulebook itself, for everyone it applies to. Source. More

    Official wording

    LDC 16-0595 - Proposed text amendment to delete the references to transportation concurrency management from the LDC. Moved to adopt. (Ordinance 16-13)

    Passed 4 to 0.

    Didn't vote: Ken Hagan, Lesley Miller, Victor Crist.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2016 voting record, page 36. Look for roll call (RCS) 1057.
