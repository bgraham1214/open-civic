  1. Community Development District (special taxing district for a new community)

    Conduct 2nd of 2 required public hearings for amendment to development agreement for design, permitting, & construction of a segment of US Hwy 301 w/Belmont Real Estate LLC, Highland CDD Holdings Inc, Highway 301 Investors LLC, & Lennar Homes LLC

    What does “Community Development District” mean?

    A special district a developer can set up under Florida law (Chapter 190) to borrow money for a new community's roads, water and amenities. Homeowners there repay it through an extra yearly charge on their property tax bill. Source. More

    Official wording

    Conduct 2nd of 2 required public hearings for amendment to development agreement for design, permitting, & construction of a segment of US Hwy 301 w/Belmont Real Estate LLC, Highland CDD Holdings Inc, Highway 301 Investors LLC, & Lennar Homes LLC. Moved the item.

    Passed 7 to 0.
