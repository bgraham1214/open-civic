  1. Change to the future land-use map at 1005 North Kingsway Road

    HC/CPA 23-09 Comprehensive Plan Map Amendment - Proposed change on 14.47 acres more or less, from Residential-4 to Community Mixed Use-12; Location: 1005 North Kingsway Road

    • Size: about 14.47 acres
    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The county's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. Source. More

    Official wording

    HC/CPA 23-09 Comprehensive Plan Map Amendment - Proposed change on 14.47 acres more or less, from Residential-4 to Community Mixed Use-12; Location: 1005 North Kingsway Road. Motioned to continue the item until the June 12, 2025, CPA hearing.

    Passed 7 to 0.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2025 voting record, page 18. Look for roll call (RCS) 4843.

  8. Change to the Urban Service Area (where the county plans water and sewer)

    What does “Change to the wording of the long-range plan” mean?

    This changes the written policies of the county's long-range land plan, which apply across many properties rather than one. Source. More

    What does “Urban Service Area” mean?

    The boundary where the county plans to provide city-style services like water and sewer. The county aims to put 80% of new growth inside it. Moving the line lets land outside it ask for more homes or businesses. Source. More
