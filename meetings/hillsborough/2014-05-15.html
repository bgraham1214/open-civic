Building and land use (5)

New to this? How building and land decisions work

  1. Change to the future land-use map

    CPA 13-11 & CPA 13-10 Future Land Use Map (FLUM) Change - North Dale Mabry Highway & Van Dyke Road

    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The county's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. Source. More

    Official wording

    CPA 13-11 & CPA 13-10 Future Land Use Map (FLUM) Change - North Dale Mabry Highway & Van Dyke Road. Moved to continue.

    Passed 4 to 0.

    Didn't vote: Al Higginbotham, Ken Hagan, Lesley Miller.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2014 voting record, page 26. Look for roll call (RCS) 333.
