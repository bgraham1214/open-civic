Money (1)

  1. Property tax rates (millage)

    Setting the TRIM Rates

    What does “Property tax rate (millage)” mean?

    Florida property tax rates are set in “mills.” One mill is $1 of tax for every $1,000 of a property's taxable value. A home with $200,000 of taxable value pays $200 per mill. Source. More

    Official wording

    Setting the TRIM Rates. Moved to set the following proposed operating & debt service millages for the purpose of the TRIM notices, & set the date, time, & place of the first public hearing on the Hillsborough County FY 2015 budget-Countywide/nonCountywide Millages...

    Passed 6 to 0.

    Didn't vote: Al Higginbotham.

    Official record: the Clerk's 2014 voting record, page 40. Look for roll call (RCS) 479.
