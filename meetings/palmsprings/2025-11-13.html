New to this? How building and land decisions work

  1. Change to the future land-use map

    (First Reading) Ordinance No. 2025-11 - Comprehensive Plan Amendment — Urban Village Overlay:

    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The city's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. More

    Official wording

    (First Reading) Ordinance No. 2025-11 - Comprehensive Plan Amendment — Urban Village Overlay: Motion to approve proposed text amendments to the Future Land-Use Element to establish an Urban Village Overlay to generate optional performance-based incentives to promote pedestrian-oriented design in vertically mixed-use (re)development projects.
