New to this? How building and land decisions work

  1. Change to the future land-use map

    (First Reading) Ordinance No. 2025-02 - Comprehensive Plan Amendment - Mobility Plan:

    What does “Change to the future land-use map” mean?

    The city's long-range plan has a map saying how each area may be used in the future and how many homes per acre are allowed at most. This changes that map for one area. A rezoning usually comes after. More

    Official wording

    (First Reading) Ordinance No. 2025-02 - Comprehensive Plan Amendment - Mobility Plan: Motion for the approval of Ordinance No. 2025-02 to amend the Comprehensive Plan of the Village of Palm Springs to modify the Transportation and Mobility Element to adopt a Mobility Plan by reference and to add new goals, objectives and policies that are consistent with the adopted Mobility Plan and with Florida Statutes in order to establish an alternative transportation system.

    Official record: the meeting agenda.
