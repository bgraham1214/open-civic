    A group of routine items approved together in one vote, to save time. Any commissioner can pull an item out to discuss it separately. More

    CONSENT AGENDA (Matters on the Consent Agenda are not expected to require discussion. Items will be adopted by one motion. If the public wishes to speak on a matter on the Consent Agenda they must inform the City Clerk prior to the start of the meeting.
