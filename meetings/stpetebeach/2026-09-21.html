  1. Property tax rates (millage)

    Final Reading Ordinance 2026-19: Establishing the Millage Rate for Fiscal year 2027

    What does “Property tax rate (millage)” mean?

    Florida property tax rates are set in “mills.” One mill is $1 of tax for every $1,000 of a property's taxable value. A home with $200,000 of taxable value pays $200 per mill. Source. More

    The city agrees to pay a company or group for goods or work. ITB (invitation to bid) and RFP (request for proposals) are the ways the city asks companies to compete for the job. More
