    What does “Incentive or tax break for a business” mean?

    Public money or a tax break offered to a company, usually in exchange for promised jobs or investment. Under Florida law a county can exempt a new or expanding business from part of its property taxes for up to 10 years, once voters have approved that power in a referendum.     What does “Property tax rate (millage)” mean?

    Florida property tax rates are set in “mills.” One mill is $1 of tax for every $1,000 of a property's taxable value. A home with $200,000 of taxable value pays $200 per mill. Source. More
