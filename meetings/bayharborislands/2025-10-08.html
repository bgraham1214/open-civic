    Official wording

    Discussion for the purpose of hearing public comments with respect to an application for a variance from Section 23-12(15)(a)(iii) of the Town Code to allow encroachments into the required street-side setback on a non-waterfront single-family property, where the Code does not permit any encroachments. The applicant is requesting two variances: the first to allow the construction of a flat roof with a total width of 18 feet 2 inches, of which 15 feet would encroach into the street-side setback; and the second to allow the construction of a second-floor balcony with a total width of 9 feet 4 inches, which would project 6 feet 2 inches into the same setback. Both proposed structures exceed the allowable building envelope and therefore require approval of the variance in order to proceed. The application is being submitted by the property owner Brian and Brittany Riemer for the property located at 9625 Broadview Drive, Bay Harbor Islands, Florida.
