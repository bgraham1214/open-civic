Money (4)

  1. Property tax rates (millage): $1,000

    Announcement of the proposed FY 2026 property tax rate of 3.4583 mills per $1,000 taxable value

    What does “Property tax rate (millage)” mean?

    Florida property tax rates are set in “mills.” One mill is $1 of tax for every $1,000 of a property's taxable value. A home with $200,000 of taxable value pays $200 per mill. Source. More

    Official wording

    Announcement of the proposed FY 2026 property tax rate of 3.4583 mills per $1,000 taxable value. The "rolled-back" millage rate is 2.9836 mills per $1,000 taxable value. The proposed FY 2026 Millage Rate is 15.91% more than the rolled-back rate, needed to fund expenditures and expenses for the Fiscal Year beginning October 1, 2025, and ending September 30, 2026. Enclosed are supporting documents.

    Official record: the meeting agenda.
