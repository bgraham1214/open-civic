How these lists are made

  • Amount approved is the total of the figures the agenda wording names for the company in decisions that passed: usually a contract's ceiling ("not to exceed"), not money paid. It includes money added to contracts approved earlier (change orders, amendments, added spending authority). Close-outs and decreases aren't added.
  • Contracts counts each decision that passed and awards the company a contract, task order, work order or purchase, including renewals. A decision that only adds money to a contract approved earlier is not counted as a contract; the number of those is shown as "increases". An amendment whose wording doesn't say it adds money is counted as a contract.
  • When one decision names several companies (a pool of firms on call), each one counts it.
  • Only governments whose votes we have can be counted, because only there do we know a decision passed. Governments whose pages show agendas without votes aren't in these lists yet.
  • Names are matched by fixed rules, so a company that changed its name, or that two governments spell very differently, can appear twice. Each company's page lists every decision with its official record.
