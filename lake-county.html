Lake County: local government records

Lake County · Clermont · Eustis · Lady Lake · Leesburg · Mount Dora · Tavares

The Lake County government decides zoning, roads and county taxes outside city limits, and county-wide services everywhere. Clermont, Eustis, Lady Lake, Leesburg, Mount Dora and Tavares each have their own city government. City councils and commissions decide zoning, city services and city taxes inside city limits. Other governments in Lake County aren't here yet: see what we cover in Florida.
