Golf carts on public roads

Ordinances and resolutions allowing or limiting golf carts and low-speed vehicles on public roads.

23 governments we follow put 56 items about this on their agendas since January 2025; a vote passed on 14.

Found by fixed rules from each item's official wording (how). Newest first, by government. "On the agenda" means no vote is recorded online, which for many governments we follow is always the case. Follow this subject (RSS feed).

Each item is marked with its kind: Talks, studies and directions, Golf carts for one event, Parking for golf carts, Single properties and businesses, Where golf carts may drive, Other items. "Passed" means a vote passed on that item, not that a law took effect.
