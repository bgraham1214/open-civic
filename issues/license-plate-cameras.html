License-plate cameras

Automated license-plate readers (Flock and others): contracts, renewals, cancellations, policies and reports.

37 governments we follow put 60 items about this on their agendas since January 2025; a vote passed on 6.

Found by fixed rules from each item's official wording (how). Newest first, by government. "On the agenda" means no vote is recorded online, which for many governments we follow is always the case. Follow this subject (RSS feed).

Each item is marked with its kind: Talks, reports and directions, Ending or limiting cameras, Local rules on their use, Contracts, purchases and permissions, Other items. "Passed" means a vote passed on that item, not that a law took effect.
