Data centers

Pauses (moratoriums), bans, zoning rules and workshops on building data centers.

31 governments we follow put 89 items about this on their agendas since January 2025; a vote passed on 24.

Found by fixed rules from each item's official wording (how). Newest first, by government. "On the agenda" means no vote is recorded online, which for many governments we follow is always the case. Follow this subject (RSS feed).

Each item is marked with its kind: Talks, studies and directions, Resolutions stating a position, Tax breaks and incentives, Single projects, Bans, Pauses (moratoriums), Zoning and building rules, Other items. "Passed" means a vote passed on that item, not that a law took effect.
