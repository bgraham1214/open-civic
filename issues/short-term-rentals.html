Short-term and vacation rentals

Registration, inspection and limits on short-term and vacation rentals.

22 governments we follow put 59 items about this on their agendas since January 2025; a vote passed on 11.

Found by fixed rules from each item's official wording (how). Newest first, by government. "On the agenda" means no vote is recorded online, which for many governments we follow is always the case. Follow this subject (RSS feed).

Each item is marked with its kind: Talks, studies and directions, Registration fees, Single properties, Monitoring software and contracts, Registration and rental rules, Other items. "Passed" means a vote passed on that item, not that a law took effect.
