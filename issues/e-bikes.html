E-bikes and scooters

Rules for electric bikes, scooters and other small electric vehicles, and studies of their safety.

33 governments we follow put 105 items about this on their agendas since January 2025; a vote passed on 13.

Found by fixed rules from each item's official wording (how). Newest first, by government. "On the agenda" means no vote is recorded online, which for many governments we follow is always the case. Follow this subject (RSS feed).

Each item is marked with its kind: Talks, studies and directions, Asking the state, Riding rules and fines, Other items. "Passed" means a vote passed on that item, not that a law took effect.
