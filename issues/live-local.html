Live Local Act housing

Projects, tax exemptions and local rules under the state's Live Local Act, which lets affordable housing be built where zoning otherwise wouldn't allow it.

42 governments we follow put 97 items about this on their agendas since January 2025; a vote passed on 34.

Found by fixed rules from each item's official wording (how). Newest first, by government. "On the agenda" means no vote is recorded online, which for many governments we follow is always the case. Follow this subject (RSS feed).
