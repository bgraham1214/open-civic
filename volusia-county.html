Volusia County: local government records

Daytona Beach Shores · DeLand · Deltona · Holly Hill · Port Orange (Volusia County)

Daytona Beach Shores, DeLand, Deltona, Holly Hill and Port Orange each have their own city government. City councils and commissions decide zoning, city services and city taxes inside city limits. Also in this county: Edgewater, Oak Hill and New Smyrna Beach. Other governments in Volusia County aren't here yet: see what we cover in Florida.
