Plant City (Hillsborough County)

Plant City has its own city government. City councils and commissions decide zoning, city services and city taxes inside city limits. Also in this county: Hillsborough County Commission, Temple Terrace. Other governments in Hillsborough County aren't here yet: see what we cover in Florida.
