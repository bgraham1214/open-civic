Broward County: local government records

Broward County · Coconut Creek · Dania Beach · Fort Lauderdale · Hallandale Beach · Hillsboro Beach · Hollywood · Lauderdale-by-the-Sea · Margate · Miramar · Parkland · Pembroke Park · Wilton Manors

The Broward County government decides zoning, roads and county taxes outside city limits, and county-wide services everywhere. Coconut Creek, Dania Beach, Fort Lauderdale, Hallandale Beach, Hillsboro Beach, Hollywood, Lauderdale-by-the-Sea, Margate, Miramar, Parkland, Pembroke Park and Wilton Manors each have their own city government. City councils and commissions decide zoning, city services and city taxes inside city limits. Other governments in Broward County aren't here yet: see what we cover in Florida.
