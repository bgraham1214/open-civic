Pinellas County: local government records

Pinellas County · Belleair · Clearwater · Pinellas Park · Seminole · St. Pete Beach · Tarpon Springs

The Pinellas County government decides zoning, roads and county taxes outside city limits, and county-wide services everywhere. Belleair, Clearwater, Pinellas Park, Seminole, St. Pete Beach and Tarpon Springs each have their own city government. City councils and commissions decide zoning, city services and city taxes inside city limits. Other governments in Pinellas County aren't here yet: see what we cover in Florida.
