Southeast Volusia city records

Edgewater · Oak Hill · New Smyrna Beach (Volusia County)

Edgewater, Oak Hill and New Smyrna Beach each have their own city government. City councils and commissions decide zoning, city services and city taxes inside city limits.

What we collect from the City of Edgewater

City Council meetings (regular, special and workshops) and the city's boards, from Edgewater's Legistar, with agenda items, actions and each council member's vote, from 2025. Code Enforcement/Special Magistrate hearings (private residents' cases) are left out. Agendas and minutes are linked on the city's site (its file server asks automated tools not to copy them).
