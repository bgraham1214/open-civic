Marion County: local government records

Marion County · Ocala

The Marion County government decides zoning, roads and county taxes outside city limits, and county-wide services everywhere. Ocala has its own city government. City councils and commissions decide zoning, city services and city taxes inside city limits. Other governments in Marion County aren't here yet: see what we cover in Florida.
