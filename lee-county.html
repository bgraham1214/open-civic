Lee County: local government records

Lee County · Fort Myers Beach

The Lee County government decides zoning, roads and county taxes outside city limits, and county-wide services everywhere. Fort Myers Beach has its own city government. City councils and commissions decide zoning, city services and city taxes inside city limits. Other governments in Lee County aren't here yet: see what we cover in Florida.
