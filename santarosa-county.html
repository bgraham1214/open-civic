Santa Rosa County: local government records

Santa Rosa County · Milton

The Santa Rosa County government decides zoning, roads and county taxes outside city limits, and county-wide services everywhere. Milton has its own city government. City councils and commissions decide zoning, city services and city taxes inside city limits. Other governments in Santa Rosa County aren't here yet: see what we cover in Florida.
