Nassau County: local government records

Nassau County · Fernandina Beach

The Nassau County government decides zoning, roads and county taxes outside city limits, and county-wide services everywhere. Fernandina Beach has its own city government. City councils and commissions decide zoning, city services and city taxes inside city limits. Other governments in Nassau County aren't here yet: see what we cover in Florida.
